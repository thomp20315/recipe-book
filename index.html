Slow Cooker Coconut Red Curry Chicken & Potatoes

Make this easy slow cooker chicken curry with tender chicken, potatoes, veggies and creamy coconut red curry sauce for a flavorful dinner.

Serves 4

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 1½ lbs chicken breast or boneless (skinless chicken thighs)
  • 1 lb red baby potatoes (halved or quartered if large)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 5 oz mild red curry paste
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk
  • ½ cup chicken bone broth
  • 1 bag stir-fry vegetables (Asian-style frozen )
  • red pepper flakes (for heat)
  • additional seasonings to taste

Steps

  1. Add the Potatoes- Place the red baby potatoes in the bottom of your slow cooker, spreading them into an even layer.
  2. Add the Chicken- Place the chicken breasts or boneless, skinless chicken thighs directly on top of the potatoes.
  3. Season the Chicken- Drizzle the olive oil over the chicken. Add the salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Mix everything together until the chicken is evenly coated while keeping the potatoes at the bottom of the slow cooker.
  4. Add the Curry Ingredients- Pour in the full-fat coconut milk, mild red curry paste, and chicken bone broth. Give everything a gentle stir until the curry paste is incorporated into the liquid. Try to keep the potatoes toward the bottom of the slow cooker.
  5. Slow Cook- Cover and cook on: LOW: 4–6 hours or HIGH: 3–4 hours. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked and the potatoes are fork-tender.
  6. Add the Vegetables- About 30 minutes before serving, add the frozen stir-fry vegetables. Give everything a good stir and try to get the vegetables as submerged in the curry sauce as possible.
  7. Finish Cooking- Cover and cook for another 30 minutes, or until the vegetables are heated through and tender.
  8. Adjust the Flavor- Taste the curry and adjust the seasonings as desired. Add additional salt, black pepper, garlic powder, curry paste, or other seasonings to taste. For extra heat, sprinkle in some red pepper flakes or chili flakes.
  9. Serve- Spoon the tender chicken, potatoes, vegetables, and creamy coconut red curry sauce into bowls. Serve with jasmine rice, brown rice, cauliflower rice, or naan bread and garnish with fresh cilantro if desired. Serve and Enjoy!

High Protein Green Chili Chicken Cornbread Meal Prep

This Green Chili Chicken Cornbread Meal Prep combines creamy, cheesy green chili chicken with a sweet and savory cornbread topping for a comforting meal that feels anything but boring. Made with rotisserie chicken, Greek yogurt, corn, green chilis, and a honey cornbread topping, these individual meal prep bowls are easy to make and packed with protein.

Serves 4Prep 15 minCook 40 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 16 oz cooked chicken, shredded
  • 4 tbsp chipotle pepper sauce (can use chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, blended)
  • 4 tbsp taco seasoning (I use Siete)
  • 1 1/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 4 tbsp light cream cheese
  • 1 cup corn
  • 1 tsp fresh lime juice
  • 4 tbsp salsa verde
  • 2 tbsp diced green chilis
  • 1/2 cup low-fat cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup honey cornbread mix (I use Jiffy)
  • 1/4 cup low-fat cottage cheese, blended (I use Good Culture)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1-2 fresh jalapeños (optional)
  • 1 tsp butter, melted (optional, for serving)
  • 1 tbsp honey (optional, for serving)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F.
  2. Soften the cream cheese. Grease four oven-safe meal prep containers that hold at least 26 ounces each.
  3. Divide the softened cream cheese, Greek yogurt, green salsa, chipotle pepper sauce, taco seasoning, and lime juice between the containers. Mix until smooth and well combined.
  4. Add the shredded chicken and corn to each container and mix until evenly coated in the creamy sauce.
  5. In a separate bowl, combine the honey cornbread mix, green chilis, shredded cheddar cheese, egg, and blended cottage cheese. Mix until just combined.
  6. Divide the cornbread mixture evenly between the four containers, spreading it over the chicken mixture. You should have approximately 1/3 cup of cornbread batter per container.
  7. If desired, top each with sliced fresh jalapeños.
  8. Place the containers on a baking sheet and bake at 375°F for 40–50 minutes, or until the cornbread topping is cooked through and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. If desired, melt the butter and mix it with the honey. Drizzle or brush a little over each serving before eating.
  10. Let the meal prep bowls cool before covering and refrigerating.

1-Pot Creamy Sausage Couscous

Serves 6Prep 10 minCook 20 minTotal 30 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 4 links Chicken Sausage
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • ½ White Onion, diced
  • 4 cloves Garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup Sun-Dried Tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup Couscous
  • 2½ cups Chicken Broth (Add more as needed)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Pepper
  • 1 tsp Red Pepper Flakes
  • 1 cup Spinach
  • ¼ cup Greek Yogurt
  • ⅛ cup Parmesan

Steps

  1. Slice your chicken sausage into ¼" pieces. In a pan on medium heat, brown the sausage on both sides. Remove the sausage from the pan and set aside.
  2. Add in your onion and garlic to the same pan and cook until fragrant (1-2 minutes).
  3. Dice your sun-dried tomatoes and add them to the same pan. Let this cook for an additional minute.
  4. Add in the couscous and let this crisp up for another minute.
  5. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil. Once the boil starts to happen, bring the pan down to a simmer and cover until fully cooked (should take 10-15 minutes). You can add more or less broth depending on how you like your couscous.
  6. Once this is nearly done cooking, add in the spinach, parmesan, and seasonings. Stir until fully combined and the spinach wilted down.
  7. Once this has finished cooking, add in your greek yogurt and stir to combine again. You can add more or less depending on how creamy you'd like your dish.
  8. Add back in the sausage, top with more parmesan, and enjoy!

30 Minute Bang Bang Chicken

This 30-minute Bang Bang Chicken is a quick, high-protein dinner made with tender seasoned chicken thighs tossed in a creamy, spicy-sweet sriracha bang bang sauce. Served over rice with crunchy cabbage slaw and green onions, it’s an easy weeknight bowl packed with bold flavor and heat.

Serves 6Prep 20 minCook 10 minTotal 30 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs, (cubed)
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/3 cup mayo
  • 3 tbsp sriracha
  • 1/4 cup Thai sweet chili sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Served with cauliflower rice or regular rice
  • Cabbage slaw
  • Freshly sliced green onions

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-sized cubes and place them in a large bowl. Add the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and olive oil. Toss until the chicken is evenly coated.
  2. Preheat a pan over medium-high heat and drizzle in a little avocado oil. Once hot, add the chicken and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fully cooked and golden on the edges.
  3. While the chicken cooks, make the bang bang sauce. In a small bowl, mix together the mayo, sriracha, Thai sweet chili sauce, rice vinegar, and garlic powder. Taste and adjust by adding more chili sauce if you like extra heat or sweetness.
  4. Once the chicken is cooked through, pour in all but a few tablespoons of the sauce and stir to coat. Let it simmer for a couple of minutes to thicken and get slightly crispy around the edges.
  5. To assemble, start with a base of cauliflower rice or regular rice. Add a handful of cabbage slaw, then spoon the bang bang chicken on top. Drizzle with the reserved sauce and garnish with sliced green onions. Enjoy!

5 Minute Dinner Prep: Salsa Verde Chicken Bake

🌶️🍗🌽5 Minute Dinner Prep: Salsa Verde Chicken Bake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked long grain white rice
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 can black beans, drained + rinsed
  • 1 cup corn (frozen or canned, drained)
  • 1 jar salsa verde
  • 4 chicken breasts (I cut mine thin)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, cumin, paprika
  • 4–6 oz cream cheese
  • Shredded Monterey Jack cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. In a 9x13 casserole dish, add rice, chicken broth, salsa verde, black beans, and corn. Stir to combine.
  3. Nestle chicken into the dish and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, cumin, and paprika. Top with cream cheese.

  5. Remove from oven, shred chicken, and stir everything together.
  6. Top with shredded Monterey Jack cheese and bake or broil for 5–10 minutes, until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Serve with:
  8. Avocado, jalapeños, and fresh cilantro.

Sloppy Joe Sweet Potato Boats

Tender roasted sweet potatoes, homemade sloppy joe filling, and melted mozzarella come together in these Sloppy Joe Sweet Potato Boats for a comforting dinner with a balanced twist. They're an easy high-protein meal that's great for weeknight dinners, meal prep, or anyone looking for a healthier take on a nostalgic family favorite.

Serves 4Prep 15 minCook 35 minTotal 50 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, (sliced lengthwise into 1-inch slices)
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1/2 small yellow onion, (diced)
  • 1 green bell pepper, (diced)
  • 3/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 6-8 oz shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Pickle slices
  • Pickled red onion
  • Pickled jalapeños

Steps

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 400℉.
  2. Poke holes with a fork into both of your sweet potatoes. Place in the microwave for 2 minutes. This will make the cutting process much easier.
  3. Once out of the microwave, slice the sweet potato into 1-inch thick slices lengthwise. I usually get about 4-5 slices of each sweet potato. Add to a baking sheet, spray with some avocado oil, and season with some salt and pepper. Place in the oven for 20-25 minutes until they can easily be pierced with a fork.
  4. Meanwhile, prep the sloppy joe meat mixture. Preheat a large pan to medium-high heat. Add the avocado oil and then the ground beef. Cook for 7-10 minutes until fully cooked.
  5. Once the meat has cooked, add the diced bell pepper and onion. Saute for 3-5 minutes until soft.
  6. Next, add the ketchup, worcestershire, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, dijon mustard, and seasonings. Mix well and bring to a low simmer. Simmer for 5-10 minutes until the sweet potatoes are ready. Taste the mixture and add salt and pepper as necessary.
  7. Once the sweet potatoes are done, place the cooked meat mixture on top of the sweet potatoes and sprinkle with cheese. Put them back in the oven for about 5 minutes until the cheese melts.
  8. Broil for the last minute or so until the cheese starts to get brown.
  9. Serve immediately with your favorite toppings! I chose some pickled red onions and pickled jalapenos. Enjoy!

ABC Baby Muffins (Apple, Banana and Carrot!)

These are deliberately very moist (and moister than regular muffins) to ensure they are easy for baby to chew and swallow, so expect that from the interior. They have three types of produce in the mix and are a yummy breakfast or snack.

Serves 12Prep 10 minCook 16 minTotal 26 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed very ripe banana ((about 2-3 small bananas))
  • ½ cup shredded apple ((about 1 small apple))
  • ¼ cup shredded carrot ((about 1 small carrot))
  • ¾ cup milk
  • ¼ cup melted unsalted butter ((slightly cooled))
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degree F and grease a 24 cup mini muffin pan with nonstick spray.
  2. Place the banana, apple, carrot, milk, butter, egg, and vanilla into a medium bowl. Stir together.
  3. Gently, but thoroughly, stir in the flour, oats, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Divide batter among the prepared muffin pan, filling each cup to the edge. (You'll use about 2 tablespoons batter in each mini muffin cup).
  5. Bake for 16-20 minutes or until a cake tester inserted into the center comes out cleanly. (I do 18 minutes in my oven.)
  6. Let cool for a few minutes in the pan, then transfer to a wire rack to cool fully. Dice up to serve to a baby eating finger foods or offer one whole to a baby eating baby led weaning-style foods.

Apple Cider Margaritas

Welcome to EPISODE 6 of my series ‘BIG BATCH’!

Ingredients

  • 16 oz tequila
  • 8 oz cointreau or triple sec
  • 8 oz lime juice
  • 10 oz apple cider
  • 4 oz water
  • 4-6 oz maple syrup
  • Apple slices
  • Cinnamon sticks
  • Rosemary Sprig

Steps

Brown Butter Cinnamon Rolls

These brown butter cinnamon rolls are the perfect Fall twist on one of my favorite recipes! Brown butter is used in both the dough and filling, and of course, it's all topped with a brown butter cream cheese icing. It's a drool-worthy combination that's sure to become a Fall staple!

Serves 12Prep 30 minCook 26 minTotal 66 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 2 cups butter unsalted, cold and cut into Tbsp-sized pieces (454g)
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour (520g)
  • 4 1/2 tsp (2 packets) instant dry yeast (14g, 1/2 oz.)
  • 1 tsp fine salt (6g)
  • 1 cup whole milk (240g)
  • 1/2 cup brown butter, melted (113g)
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar (100g)
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature (112g)
  • 3/4 cup brown butter, melted (170g)
  • 1 1/4 cups packed light brown sugar (250g)
  • 2 Tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream - to be added later (45g)
  • 1/4 cup brown butter, room temperature (56g)
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese, room temperature (56g)
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste (10g)
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
  • 1 cup powdered sugar (125g)
  • 2 Tbsp heavy cream or milk, room temperature (30g)

Steps

  1. The first step is to brown the butter. Get a heatproof bowl out to pour the butter into once it's browned. Then place 2 cups of unsalted butter in a large, light-colored frying pan (to make it easier to see) over medium heat. Stir the butter the entire time with a rubber spatula to prevent the milk solids from burning!
  2. Once melted, the butter will begin to foam and sizzle around the edges. Keep stirring!! The butter should turn golden brown after 5–6 minutes and be topped with a foamy layer of bubbles. Shortly after this, the foam should kind of fade away, and the milk solids at the bottom of the pan will be a perfectly toasted brown. It should have a strong, nutty aroma.
  3. Immediately remove the pan from heat and pour the butter into a heatproof bowl to stop the cooking process. If left in the hot pan, the milk solids will burn. It can happen in a flash, trust me!! Set aside.
  4. In a large bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer, combine 4 cups of flour, 2 packets of instant dry yeast, and 1 tsp fine salt.
  5. In a separate bowl, combine 1 cup milk, 1/2 cup melted brown butter, and 1/2 cup granulated sugar. Heat for 30-60 seconds in the microwave and stir to combine the ingredients. The mixture should be warm but not hot to the touch (otherwise it can kill the yeast!).
  6. Slowly pour the warm milk mixture into the flour mixture and mix on medium-low speed with a dough hook or by hand. Scrape around the edges with a rubber spatula to make sure all the flour gets incorporated.
  7. Add 2 large eggs, one at a time. Mix on medium speed until a sticky dough ball forms. Continue to mix the dough on medium speed for a couple of minutes until the dough becomes more elastic and looks smooth.
  8. Then mix in additional flour, 1 Tbsp at a time, on a low speed until the dough is tacky but no longer sticky (I usually add 1-2 additional Tbsp of flour). It should pull away/no longer stick to the sides of your mixing bowl once it's ready to rest. Another way to test the dough is to tap it with your finger. It should be tacky, but shouldn't stick to your finger or leave any residue on your finger.
  9. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and set aside to let the dough rest for 10-20 minutes to allow the gluten to relax. This will make it easier to roll out.
  10. While the dough rests, make the cinnamon roll filling. Add 3/4 cup melted brown butter, 1 1/4 cups packed brown sugar, 2 Tbsp ground cinnamon, and 1/2 tsp fine salt to a medium-sized bowl. Mix together until combined, then set aside. The mixture should have a thick, paste-like consistency. Set aside.
  11. Preheat the oven to 200°F/93°C (to help the cinnamon rolls rise), and line a metal 9x13-inch pan. If you're making these the day before, don't preheat the oven. Set aside.
  12. Place the dough on a lightly floured surface and roll it into a large rectangle that's about 24 in x 18 in. Spread 2/3 of the filling in a thin layer across the dough with an offset spatula or butter knife.
  13. Mix 1/4 cup of heavy cream into the remaining filling, then spread it onto the bottom of the prepared 9x13-inch pan to give the cinnamon rolls deliciously sticky bottoms.
  14. Use a rolling pastry cutter or pizza cutter to cut 12 equal strips that are about 2" wide - this is why it's important that the dough is 24" wide! If it's shorter, you may need to cut your strips a bit narrower. Then roll each cinnamon roll up individually. Place them in the prepared 9 in x 13 in pan. You can also roll the dough into a log and cut each piece with a knife or dental floss if you prefer!
  15. If you are making these cinnamon rolls the night before, cover the cinnamon rolls tightly with plastic wrap and place them in the fridge overnight. The following morning, take them out of the fridge and preheat the oven to 200°F/93°C. Then turn off the oven, cover the rolls with foil, and place the rolls in the oven for about 30 minutes, until they've risen.
  16. If you make these the morning of, turn off the oven, cover the rolls with foil, and place them in the oven to rise for about 30 minutes. MAKE SURE YOU'VE TURNED THE OVEN OFF. We want a nice cozy place for the rolls to rise, but we don't want to bake them yet.
  17. At this point, they should look puffier and have grown about twice in size. Preheat the oven to 350°F/175°C. Then bake the rolls for 24-28 minutes, or until they're just starting to brown. Rotate the pan halfway through to help them bake evenly.
  18. While the cinnamon rolls rise and bake, make the brown butter cream cheese icing. Mix 1/4 cup softened brown butter, 1/4 cup room-temperature cream cheese, 2 tsp vanilla extract, and 1/4 tsp salt together with a spoon or electric hand mixer.
  19. Then mix in 1 cup of powdered sugar and 2 Tbsp of heavy cream or milk at a low speed until it is smooth.
  20. Let the cinnamon rolls cool for about 10 minutes, then spoon the icing over them and enjoy warm! Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for a few hours, or in the fridge for up to 5 days.

Chewy Pumpkin Cookies

These chewy pumpkin cookies have the perfect amount of pumpkin spice and each bite truly melts in your mouth. With chewy centers and spiced sugar topping, you will not believe how delicious these fall cookies are!

Serves 18Prep 30 minCook 12 min

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated white sugar
  • 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 3/4 cup (168 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup (220 g) light brown sugar, packed
  • 2 egg yolks, at room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 cup (122 g) canned pumpkin puree (Libby's works best)
  • 1 3/4 cups (219 g) all purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Steps

  1. In a small bowl mix the granulated sugar and pumpkin pie spice together. Set aside.
  2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  3. Start by drying your pumpkin. (This step is not optional. The cookies will not bake properly if you skip this step.) Spread the canned pumpkin on a plate and lightly press with a paper towel to absorb the excess liquid. Repeat the step at least four more times. The pumpkin should be dry enough that it goes from being 1/2 cup dried down to just about a 1/4 cup. Hardly any liquid should transfer onto a paper towel once it has been dried enough.(Like stated above, try to use Libby's canned pumpkin as almost all cans of it have the same consistency and are not too wet.) Then set aside.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, pumpkin pie spice, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Set aside.
  5. In a large bowl cream the softened butter and brown sugar together with an electric mixer on high speed for 1-2 minutes until light and fluffy.
  6. Add in the egg yolks and vanilla and mix on medium speed until pale and fluffy, about 1-2 minutes.
  7. Add in the pumpkin and mix on medium-low speed to combine.
  8. Add in the dry ingredients and mix on low speed just until combined.
  9. Scoop the dough with a 2 tbsp cookie scoop, and roll them into balls. Then roll the dough balls in the spiced sugar. (If the dough is too "sticky" chill it in the fridge for 10 minutes, then proceed.)
  10. Place the cookie dough balls at least 2 inches apart on the baking sheets. (I usually bake 6 at a time.)
  11. Bake the cookies for 12-14 minutes. (12 minutes for really chewy centers, 14 minutes for a slightly crispier cookie). When the cookies are done baking the centers will be puffy. As they cool the centers will fall and the cookie will get "wrinkly".
  12. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer them to a cooling rack to finish cooling. They are best enjoyed when they have cooled for at least 15 minutes!

Cream Cheese Sugar Cookies

This is the best sugar cookie recipe for Christmas! These cream cheese sugar cookies bake into a deliciously soft and buttery texture, but still hold their shape when baked. The dough only chills for one hour, or it can be prepped the day before and chill overnight. Spread some cream cheese buttercream on top for easy decorating. It dries firm, so these cookies can be stacked and stored - a must f

Serves 26Prep 2 hr Cook 10 minTotal 2 hr 30 min

Family ratings

Mom
Dad
Kids

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (224g) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 6 oz (170g) full fat cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1 1/2 cups (300g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
  • 3 1/2 cups (450g) all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled, plus more for dusting
  • 1/2 cup (112g) unsalted butter, room temp
  • 2 oz full fat cream cheese, room temp
  • 2 cups (220g) powdered sugar, spooned and leveled
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Gel food coloring (optional)
  • Sprinkles, for decorating

Steps

  1. In a large bowl using a hand or stand mixer with the paddle attachment, mix the butter, cream cheese, and sugar on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 1 minute.
  2. Scrape down the bowl and mix in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Scrape down the bowl and mix in the salt, baking powder, and cream of tartar until combined.
  4. Last, mix in the flour. When your mixer starts to struggle with the thick dough, remove from the mixer and use a rubber spatula to ensure the dough is well combined. It’s VERY thick and sticky, so this requires a little elbow grease!
  5. Dump the dough out onto a sheet of plastic wrap lightly dusted with flour.
  6. Coat your hands in flour and pat the dough out into a 1” thick rectangle. Wrap and chill for at least 1 hour or overnight.
  7. When ready, preheat the oven to 350F and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
  8. Roll the dough out onto a floured surface to ¼” thick (we want nice and thick cookies).
  9. Cut out as many cookies as you can and place them about an inch apart on the baking sheet. With any leftover cut out cookies, store in the refrigerator.
  10. Bake for 8-10 minutes (9 was the sweet spot for me), or until they look dry on the surface but just barely done in the middle. In the meantime, you can re-roll the dough scraps, continuing to store any cut out cookies in the refrigerator until it’s time to bake them.
  11. Let the cookies rest on the pan for about a minute, then transfer to a cooling rack before baking the rest of the batch.
  12. In a large bowl using a hand or stand mixer with the paddle attachment, mix the butter and cream cheese until smooth.
  13. Mix in the powdered sugar one cup at a time, followed by the vanilla. It should be thick and smooth. Mix in a few drops of gel food coloring if desired.
  14. Spread the frosting on the cooled cookies and immediately top with sprinkles. If you wait too long, the sprinkles won’t stick so frost one cookie, add sprinkles, then move on to the next.
  15. Now they’re ready to enjoy!
